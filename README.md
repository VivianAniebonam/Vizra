# 🌍 Vizra — AI-Driven Gig Platform

**Vizra Technologies Inc.** is a registered **Canadian tech company** building an **AI-powered gig platform** that connects households and businesses with **verified, trusted service providers** such as caregivers, babysitters, cleaners, PSWs, and tech helpers.

Our goal is to empower communities through **digital innovation** — making it easy for families, organizations, and workers to connect safely, quickly, and transparently.

---

## Project Overview

Vizra is **60% AI-powered**, automating core processes such as:
- 🤖 Intelligent job matching using AI scoring
- 🔒 Identity & document verification
- 🕒 Smart scheduling and shift optimization
- 💬 Real-time communication between clients and job seekers
- 💵 Secure payments with transparency and fairness

Unlike traditional marketplaces, Vizra is **built for trust, compliance, and inclusion**, aligning with Canada’s innovation and workforce development goals.
